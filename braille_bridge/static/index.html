Core Architecture

Built for Real-World Tactile Accessibility

A unified ecosystem linking multi-language translation, document processing, and embedded physical hardware.

🔤

Bilingual Translation Engine

Supports Unified English Braille (UEB) Grade 1 & Grade 2 contractions and Bharati Braille Hindi with Devanagari vowels, consonants, and single-cell conjuncts.

📄

Multi-Page PDF & Dual OCR

High-capacity document processing up to 200,000 characters with selective page range extraction and fallback to Windows Media Runtime and Tesseract OCR.

⚙️

ESP32 Physical Actuator

Interfaces with a single-character 6-servo Braille cell via PCA9685 I2C controller, tactile advance button (GPIO 32), and buzzer (GPIO 25).

🧠

Adaptive Leitner Learning

SQLite-backed 3-box spaced repetition system with 6 tiered curricula across Beginner, Intermediate, and Advanced, plus targeted error revision.

Workflow

How Braille Bridge Works

From digital content to tactile sensory feedback in three seamless stages.

1

Ingest & Input

Type text, speak dictation hands-free, upload multi-page PDFs, or snap a photo of a printed page.

2

Rule-Based Translation

High-throughput Python engine translates 5,000 chars in 18.4ms into 6-dot Unicode, ASCII Braille, and BRF files.

3

Tactile Actuation & Feedback

Step through characters on the physical ESP32 6-dot cell or practice with the interactive virtual mechanical simulator.
